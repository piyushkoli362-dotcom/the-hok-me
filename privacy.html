Privacy notice.

How project inquiries and collaboration interest are handled.

This notice describes the current website inquiry flow. The agency owner should confirm the actual operating entity, contact channel, retention period and applicable privacy requirements before launch.

Information collected

The project inquiry and collaboration-interest forms collect your name, email, required phone number and consent. Project inquiries also collect your organization, website or social link, requested services, budget range, preferred contact method and project message. Collaboration interests also collect the selected discipline, skills, links, motivation, availability and preferred engagement. Some technical security measures may process an HMAC of the connecting address for rate limiting; the raw address is not saved in the application database.

Why it is collected

Submission details are used to review and respond to project or collaboration requests. A collaboration-interest submission is not a job application or offer. Submitting a form does not create a service agreement or guarantee a response.

Where it is stored

During the local preview, submissions are stored in a private workbook and source record on the preview computer. On a configured public deployment, validated submissions are stored in the agency’s private PostgreSQL database. A private Google Sheets copy is optional and is queued only when the owner configures it. Online submissions are disabled if secure database storage is unavailable. The public site build does not include the local submission workbook.

Retention and deletion

Rate-limit hashes expire after 24 hours. Inquiry retention and deletion schedules still need to be set by the agency owner. This notice does not yet publish a verified privacy contact or dedicated deletion-request workflow; please add one before public launch.

Other data

Hosting providers may process standard technical logs to deliver and protect the website. Configure a verified privacy contact and applicable data-processing disclosures before accepting live inquiries.
